Meet A. Nox

A private manuscript, one persistent friend, and a fictional institution that refused to stay on the page.

From Lafayette to Grimwood.

Born and raised in Lafayette, Louisiana, A. Nox now lives in Oklahoma City. Between working in the medical field and raising three children, she found a creative home in dark romance.

Her first manuscript wasn't written for an audience. It stayed private until a close friend encouraged her to publish, turning a story she loved into the beginning of the Deranged Darlings series.

Her fiction thrives on fractured perspectives, devotion taken too far, and women whose motives are impossible to pin down. Trust the heroine if you want. Just don't expect the evidence to agree.

Meet A. Nox

Dark romance for readers who know the woman can be the monster, the warning, and the love story.

Small-town roots. Unhinged heroines.

A. Nox was born and raised in Lafayette, Louisiana, and now lives in Oklahoma City. A mother of three who works in the medical field, she fell in love with dark romance and began writing the story that refused to leave her head.

Psycho began as a private escape with no plans for publication. Encouragement from a close friend changed that. A. Nox chose to trust the story—and the future stories she felt born to tell.

Her obsession is role reversal: female main characters who are every bit as dangerous, possessive, and unhinged as the men beside them. In an A. Nox story, she is not the victim waiting to be saved. She is the red flag.
